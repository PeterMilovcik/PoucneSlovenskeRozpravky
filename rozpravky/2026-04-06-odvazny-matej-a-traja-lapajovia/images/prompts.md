# Prompty pre ilustrácie: O odvážnom Matejovi a troch lapajoch

> **Verzia**: v1
> **Počet scén**: 13 + obálka = 14 ilustrácií
> **Konzistentný štýl** — každý prompt začína identickým štýlovým prefixom.
> **Formát**: 16:9 (wide landscape), no text in images.

## Štýlový prefix (použiť v KAŽDOM prompte)

```
Children's book watercolor illustration, soft and warm style, gentle brushstrokes, storybook aesthetic, consistent art style.
```

## Referenčné opisy postáv

**Matej**: A 12-year-old boy with messy chestnut-brown hair, bright blue eyes, wearing a simple white shirt and brown trousers, with an old silver whistle hanging on a thin leather cord around his neck. Friendly round face, determined yet kind expression.

**Babka Rozália**: An elderly grandmother with white hair tied under a blue headscarf, kind wrinkled face with warm brown eyes, wearing a traditional Slovak folk blouse and long dark skirt, knitting needles often in her hands, always with a gentle loving smile.

**Bartolomej**: A very tall and extremely thin man, lanky like a fence post, with a long face and big nose, wearing a patched oversized brown coat three sizes too big and a worn hat with holes in it. Comical and clumsy, not scary.

**Cyprián**: A short and very round man, chubby like a pinecone, with red sweaty cheeks, wearing a patched oversized brown coat three sizes too big and a worn hat with holes in it. Comical and bumbling, not scary.

**Demeter**: A medium-built man with enormous protruding ears that stick out like satellite dishes, wearing a patched oversized brown coat three sizes too big and a worn hat with holes in it. Head always slightly tilted as if listening. Comical, not scary.

**Starosta Kubo**: A sturdy middle-aged man with a large bushy brown mustache, wearing a formal vest over a white shirt, looking authoritative but kind and slightly bewildered.
