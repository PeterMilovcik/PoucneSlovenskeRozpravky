# Prompty pre ilustrácie: Tomášova zlatá minca (v3 — rozšírené na 12 scén)

> **Zmeny v3**: Dvojnásobok scén (12 namiesto 6) pre lepšie mapovanie na audio.
> Konzistentný štýl — každý prompt začína identickým štýlovým prefixom.
> Bicykle minimalizované (DALL-E limitácia). Žiadne mechanické detaily.

## Štýlový prefix (použiť v KAŽDOM prompte)

```
Soft watercolor children's book illustration in warm storybook style. Gentle rounded brushstrokes, dreamy color blending, soft edges. Warm golden and earthy color palette with touches of green and blue. Characters have friendly rounded proportions with expressive eyes. European small-town setting. Safe, magical, inviting atmosphere for children. No text, no letters, no words in the image. Wide format, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

## Referenčné opisy postáv

**Tomáš**: A young boy, about 8 years old, with wavy brown hair, bright blue eyes, wearing a simple blue-and-white striped t-shirt and brown shorts, friendly round face, slightly freckled cheeks, bare feet or simple sneakers.

**Kubko**: A small boy, about 8 years old, slightly shorter than Tomáš, with short blond hair, big round glasses, kind hazel eyes, wearing a green hoodie, shy gentle smile.

**Otec (Ocko)**: A kind-looking man in his mid-30s with short brown hair, warm smile, wearing a simple checkered shirt with rolled-up sleeves, flour on his hands from baking.

**Starý otec Jano**: An elderly man with white hair, warm brown eyes, gentle wrinkled face, wearing a simple wool vest over a checkered shirt, sawdust on his hands, kind grandfather figure.

**Zlatá minca**: A golden coin about the size of a child's palm, glowing softly with warm golden light, with a simple engraved hand symbol on one side.
