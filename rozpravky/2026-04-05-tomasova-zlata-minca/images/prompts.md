# Prompty pre ilustrácie: Tomášova zlatá minca (v2 — opravené)

> **Zmeny v2**: Minimalizované bicykle (DALL-E limitácia), jednoduchšie hračky,
> presnejšie priestorové vzťahy, menej mechanických detailov.

## Referenčné opisy postáv

**Tomáš**: A young boy, about 8 years old, with wavy brown hair, bright blue eyes, wearing a simple striped t-shirt and jeans, friendly round face, slightly freckled cheeks.

**Kubko**: A small boy, about 8 years old, slightly shorter than Tomáš, with short blond hair, big round glasses, kind hazel eyes, wearing a green hoodie, shy gentle smile.

**Starý otec Jano**: An elderly man with white hair, warm brown eyes, gentle wrinkled face, wearing a simple wool vest over a checkered shirt, sawdust on his hands, kind grandfather figure.

**Zlatá minca**: A golden coin about the size of a child's palm, glowing softly with warm golden light, with a simple engraved pattern on one side.

## Obálka (Cover)

**Moment**: Tomáš drží zlatú mincu v dlaniach, za ním mestečko pod horami
**Prompt**:
Children's book watercolor illustration, soft and warm style, gentle brushstrokes, storybook aesthetic. A young boy about 8 years old with wavy brown hair and bright blue eyes, wearing a striped t-shirt, holds a small glowing golden coin in both cupped hands close to his chest, looking down at it with wonder and curiosity. Behind him, a charming small European town with colorful houses and red rooftops nestled at the foot of tall green mountains. Warm golden afternoon light bathes the scene. The coin emits a soft magical golden glow illuminating the boy's face from below. The upper third of the image has a simple sky with soft clouds, leaving space for title. Bright, cheerful watercolor palette with golden, green, and blue tones. Safe for children, magical and inviting atmosphere. Wide format, 16:9 aspect ratio.
